CaliChat User Manual

CaliChat answers your customers on WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram with an AI agent that looks up real products and orders in your own systems. Your team can step in at any moment.

I am setting upOwner checklist: from empty workspace to live in six steps. I answer customersInbox, handoffs, notes, saved replies. Something is wrongSymptoms, causes and fixes.

Start hereWhat CaliChat does

When a customer messages your business, CaliChat reads the message, decides what information it needs, fetches it from your systems (products, stock, orders, delivery status) or from your knowledge base (policies, FAQs), and replies in the customer's language. If it cannot help, or the customer asks for a person, it hands the chat to your team.

1. Customer writesWhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram
→
2. QueueStored safely, answered in order
→
3. AI agentUses your tools and knowledge
→
4. ReplyOr hand off to your team

What the AI can and cannot do

  • Can: answer in English, Sinhala or Tamil; look up products, stock and the customer's own orders; answer policy questions from your knowledge base; remember preferences between chats; read voice messages and images (converted to text); ask for missing details such as an order number.
  • Cannot: promise refunds, discounts or price changes; see another customer's data; change anything in your systems unless you enabled a write tool and the customer confirmed; read attachments other than voice notes and images.
Good to know. The AI only states facts it gets from your tools or knowledge base. If it finds nothing, it says so and offers a human. It never makes up prices or order status.

Start hereSigning in

  1. Open the CaliChat address your administrator gave you and enter your email and password.
  2. First time? Use the one-time link you were sent. It opens a page where you choose your own password. The link works once and expires after 72 hours.
  3. If a temporary password was set for you, CaliChat asks you to change it before you continue.

Password rules

  • At least 12 characters.
  • Not a common password, and it must not contain the first part of your email address.
  • Mix letters, numbers or symbols. A long passphrase of 16+ characters is fine on its own.

Protection you will notice

Lockout
Five wrong passwords in 15 minutes lock the account for 15 minutes, even if you then type the right one. Ask an owner for a reset link if you cannot wait.
Idle timeout
You are signed out after 30 minutes without activity, and after 12 hours in any case.
Suspended workspace
If your workspace is suspended, everyone is signed out immediately.
Change password
Use Change password in the left menu, under your name.
No two-step verification yet. Sign-in uses email and password only. Use a strong, unique password.

Start hereFirst sign-in and the setup guide

A new workspace starts empty. The first time an owner signs in, CaliChat opens the Setup guide. It has seven steps, and each one turns green by itself when the work is really done, so you can stop and come back later. A progress card on the Dashboard and the Setup guide menu item take you back until everything is done or you press Hide this guide.

  1. Business profile. Name, timezone and the hours your team is available.
  2. Teach your AI agent. Describe your business in a few sentences and press Draft with AI; it writes the briefing for you to edit. Or type your own.
  3. Knowledge. Add your policies and FAQs.
  4. Connect your systems (optional). Link your store, ERP or CRM so the AI can look up and manage orders. You can skip this and add it later.
  5. Chat with your agent in the Playground, in every language you serve.
  6. Connect your channels: WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram. See One-click connect.
  7. Receive a real message. Send one from your own phone and see it arrive in the Inbox.
Who sees it. Only owners see the guide. People you invite as agents go straight to the dashboard.

Set up (owners)Setup checklist

A new workspace shows a “Get live in 4 steps” card on the dashboard. This is the full path, in the order that works best.

  1. Settings. Set your timezone, team hours and (optionally) your own Gemini key.
  2. AI Agents. Describe your business, tone and rules.
  3. Knowledge. Add your policies and FAQs.
  4. Connectors. Connect your ERP, store or CRM so the AI can look up real data.
  5. Playground. Chat as a customer and check every answer.
  6. Channels. Link WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram. Only now do real customers reach the AI.
Tip. Do the channel last. Everything before it can be tested safely in the Playground, with no customer involved.

Set up (owners)AI Agents

An agent is the persona that talks to customers. Most businesses need one. Open AI Agents then New agent.

Agent name
The first name customers see in the conversation, for example “Kavi”.
Business instructions
The most important field. Explain what you sell, prices currency, delivery areas, return rules, when to offer alternatives, and when to call a human. Write it like a briefing for a new staff member.
Tone
Friendly, professional, casual, formal or playful.
Languages
For example “English, Sinhala, Tamil”. The AI replies in the language the customer writes in.
Temperature
0 to 2. Leave at 1.0, which is recommended for the current Gemini 3 models. Lower it only if answers vary too much.
Tools
Tick the tools this agent may use. Leave all unticked to allow every active tool. Fewer tools means faster and more accurate choices.
Fallback message
Sent if the AI service is unavailable after several retries. The chat is then handed to your team.
Active
Untick to pause the agent. If no agent is active, customers get no AI replies and you receive an alert.
Writing good instructions. Be concrete: “Delivery is Rs. 350, free over Rs. 10,000. Colombo takes 1–2 days.” beats “We deliver quickly.” State rules as do and do not, and name the situations that must go to a human, such as refund disputes.

Each channel can use a different agent (see Channels). Otherwise the first active agent answers.

Set up (owners)Knowledge

The knowledge base holds the answers that are the same for every customer: returns, shipping, payment methods, opening hours, sizing guides. The AI searches it by meaning, so a customer asking in Sinhala finds an answer you wrote in English.

  1. Open Knowledge, enter a Title and paste the Content, or upload a .txt, .md or .csv file.
  2. Separate topics with a blank line. Short, single-topic paragraphs are found more reliably than long ones.
  3. Press Save & index. CaliChat splits the text into passages and builds a search index.

The document list shows semantic ✓ when meaning-based search is ready. If it says keyword only, press Build semantic index at the top of the page.

Improve it over time

The Unanswered questions panel lists what customers asked that nothing in the knowledge base matched, with a count. Add those topics and the list shrinks. The same list appears on the Monitoring page.

Do not store secrets here. Knowledge text can be shown to customers. Put prices that change often in your ERP and read them with a tool instead.

Set up (owners)Connectors and tools

A connector is a connection to one of your systems (a REST API). A tool is one question the AI is allowed to ask that system, such as “search products” or “get order status”. You decide exactly which questions are allowed and which fields come back.

1. Create the connector

  1. Open Connectors then New connector.
  2. Enter a name and the Base URL of your API, for example https://erp.example.com/api.
  3. Choose how it signs in: none, bearer token, API key in a header, basic, or a key in the URL. Enter the secret. It is stored encrypted and never displayed again.
  4. Set the timeout (3 to 30 seconds) and save.
Try it with the demo. Base URL …/mock/erp with bearer token demo-erp-token gives a sample fashion shop stored in the database: 24 products (two out of stock) and 12 orders for several customers. To see orders for your own phone, ask your administrator to run php cli/seed_demo.php <your number>, for example php cli/seed_demo.php 94771234567 (country code, digits only).

2. Add a tool

Open the connector and choose Add tool.

Tool name
Letters, numbers and underscores, for example get_order.
Method and path
For example GET /orders/{order_no}. Words in braces become parameters.
When to use it
Plain-language instruction the AI reads. Be specific: “Look up the status and tracking of THIS customer's order by order number.”
Fields the AI may see
A comma list such as order_no,status,eta,total,items,name,qty. Everything else is hidden from the AI. This is required unless you tick “Expose all fields”, which is not recommended.
Response path
If the useful data sits inside the reply, enter its path, for example data.
Cache
Seconds to reuse a result for GET tools, useful for product lists. 0 turns caching off.

3. Parameters

Each parameter is one input the tool needs. Give every one a type and a value source:

SourceMeaningUse it for
aiThe AI fills it in from the chat.Search words, order numbers.
contact.phoneForced from the customer's verified phone number. The AI cannot change it.“Show only this customer's orders.”
contact.external_id / contact.nameForced from the messaging account.Account lookups.
staticA fixed value you type.Store ID, API version.

For parameters the AI fills in, add limits: an allowed pattern (for example ^ORD-[0-9]{3,8}$), a list of allowed values, a maximum length, or a minimum and maximum for numbers. Values that break the rules are rejected before they reach your system. Values placed in the URL path may only contain letters, numbers, dots, dashes and underscores.

Protect customer data. Any tool that returns personal or order data must have a parameter with source contact.phone. Without it, a customer could ask about someone else's order. Test this in the Playground with a different phone number.

4. Test it

Open the tool in the list, fill in the test values, enter a test customer phone and press Run test. You see the status, the time and the data the AI would receive. Tests run read-only: write tools cannot be run here.

Tools that change data

For POST, PUT, PATCH and DELETE tools you must tick Allow this non-GET tool to change data. The AI then must ask the customer to confirm the exact action, and only calls the tool after a clear “yes”. Each call also carries an idempotency key so a repeated request is not applied twice. Start with read-only tools and add write tools only when you need them.

Set up (owners)AI actions and approvals

Besides answering questions, the AI can do things in your systems: place an order, change a delivery address, cancel an order, record a sales enquiry (a lead), confirm a lead. It does this on its own, in the chat, once the customer has agreed. The demo store comes with these tools ready: create_order, update_order, cancel_order, create_lead, confirm_lead and list_my_leads.

How a change happens

1. Customer asks“Please order 2 caps”
→
2. PreviewNothing changes; the AI reads the details back
→
3. Customer says yesA new message from them
→
4. Action runsThe AI reports the real result

These protections are enforced by CaliChat itself, not left to the AI's judgement:

  • Preview first. The first call to a write tool only records a preview. Nothing is created, changed or cancelled.
  • The customer must reply. The real call is accepted only after the customer sends a new message following the preview, with exactly the same details. A changed detail needs a new preview.
  • Once only. Each confirmation can be used one time, and repeated requests are not applied twice.
  • Limits. At most 6 changes per chat per hour. Your own system keeps its rules too: stock checks, order value limits, “only while Processing”, and “an order belongs to one phone number”.
  • Their records only. Tools use the verified phone number of the sender, so a customer can only change their own orders and leads.
  • No made-up results. If the AI says something is done, or quotes an order or enquiry number, CaliChat checks that a tool really returned it. If not, the AI is told to redo it, and if it still cannot, the chat is handed to your team.
  • Everything is recorded in the Audit log (ai_action) and listed on the Approvals page.

Choosing how autonomous each action is

In Connectors, open a tool that changes data. Two settings decide what happens:

Allow this tool to change data
Must be ticked or the tool cannot run. Only owners can tick it.
Require team approval
Unticked (default): the AI acts by itself after the customer's “yes”. Ticked: the AI submits the request and tells the customer a team member will confirm. Use this for risky actions such as refunds or large orders.

The Approvals page

Requests waiting for a person appear under Approvals (the menu shows a count, and an alert is raised). Approve runs the action and messages the customer with the result. Reject tells them the request could not be completed and that a team member will follow up. The page also lists the changes the AI made by itself.

Adding actions for your own system

  1. Create a connector to your API (see Connectors and tools).
  2. Add a tool with method POST, PATCH, PUT or DELETE and tick Allow this tool to change data.
  3. Describe in plain words when to use it. Define the inputs; mark the customer's phone as contact.phone. Use patterns and limits on every input.
  4. List the response fields the AI may see. Include your system's message field and the order or reference number, so the AI can report them.
  5. Make your API enforce its own rules and return a clear error text when it refuses (for example “order already shipped”): the AI passes that reason to the customer.
Test against a test copy. The Playground can run write actions for real (a checkbox turns this off). The tool test panel under Connectors never runs write tools. Use a test copy of your system, or a test channel such as the Twilio Sandbox, when trying changes.

Set up (owners)Store, Orders and Leads

No ERP or CRM? CaliChat has a small one built in. The AI agent reads and writes it exactly like an outside system, with the same safeguards from AI actions and approvals.

Store

  1. Open Store. Press Add sample products to try it, or add your own products (SKU, name, price, stock, sizes, colours) or import a CSV. The first row of a CSV must name the columns: sku,name,price,stock,category,variants,colors,description. Existing SKUs are updated.
  2. Add delivery areas: town, fee, free-over amount, delivery time, and whether cash on delivery is allowed. Towns you do not list use the standard fee.
  3. Set the store rules: standard fee, free delivery threshold, the payment methods you accept, and the largest order the AI may place. Bigger orders must be placed by your team.
  4. Press Use this store with the AI. The agent can now search products, quote delivery, place, change and cancel orders, and record leads here. Test it in the Playground with Run write actions ticked.

Your cost price is never shown to the AI or to customers. Stock is reserved when an order is placed and returned when it is cancelled.

Orders

Orders lists every order, whether the AI or your team placed it. Open one to move it along (Processing, Packed, Shipped, Delivered), add the courier and tracking number, mark it paid, change the delivery details while it is still Processing, or cancel it. Cash-on-delivery orders become Paid when delivered. Use Create an order for phone, shop or social media orders.

Leads

Leads is a board with five stages: New, Contacted, Qualified, Won, Lost. Enquiries recorded by the AI appear in New with a customer confirmed badge once the customer confirmed. Change a lead's stage from the dropdown on its card, or open it to assign an owner, set an expected value and add notes. Staff can work orders and leads; only owners change the catalogue, templates and follow-ups.

Set up (owners)WhatsApp templates

WhatsApp lets a business message a customer first, or write after 24 hours of silence, only with a template that Meta has approved. WA Templates helps you write them correctly.

  1. Under Business account, enter the WhatsApp Business Account ID of your number (Meta Business Settings, WhatsApp accounts). Numbers connected with one-click connect already have it. Twilio sandbox numbers cannot use these templates.
  2. Press Add starter templates (order confirmed, shipped, delivered, payment reminder, review request, still interested) or choose New template.
  3. Write the message with readable placeholders such as {name}, {order_no}, {courier}, {tracking}. The preview shows sample values and which {{1}}, {{2}} Meta will receive.
  4. Read the checks under the preview. Errors block submission (for example a message that starts or ends with a placeholder, or has more than three buttons). Warnings are advice (for example a marketing template without “Reply STOP to unsubscribe”).
  5. Press Submit to Meta. Approval takes minutes to a day. Press Check approval status, or wait: the background worker checks every 5 minutes and alerts you if a template is rejected, with Meta's reason.

Meta does not allow editing a template after submission. To change one, save a new version under a new name. Utility templates are for order and account updates; Marketing templates (offers, review requests, nudges) cost more and need the customer's opt-in. Campaigns can use any approved template and fill {name} and {shop}.

Set up (owners)Follow-ups

Follow-ups sends messages automatically so you do not have to remember. Press Add recommended set to create the usual ones, then adjust or switch them off.

Order placed / packed / shipped / delivered / cancelled
Sent when you change the order's status. Shipped messages can include the courier, tracking number and delivery estimate. The recommended “order placed” message goes only to orders your team placed, because the customer already confirmed AI orders in the chat.
Payment still pending
For bank transfer or card orders that are still unpaid after the delay (default 6 hours).
Ask for a review
One day after delivery. This is promotional, so only customers marked Opted in receive it.
Chat went quiet
The customer looked at products, the AI replied, then nothing for 2 hours. One gentle nudge, never after they write again, never when a person has taken over. Promotional: needs Opted in.
Lead not followed up
Alerts your team about a New lead nobody has moved forward after 4 hours.

Rules that always apply

  • 24-hour window. Free text is sent only within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After that, the follow-up needs an approved template attached; otherwise it is skipped and the log says why.
  • Consent. Promotional follow-ups need Opted in (Contacts). A customer who sent STOP still gets order updates but no promotions.
  • Quiet hours. Nothing is sent between 21:00 and 08:00 by default (change it on the page). Held messages go out when quiet hours end.
  • Checked again at sending time. A shipped message is dropped if the order was cancelled meanwhile; a nudge is dropped if the customer wrote again.
  • Never twice. Each follow-up is sent once per order, chat or lead.

The table What happened lists the latest follow-ups with the result (sent, skipped, failed, waiting) and the reason. Follow-ups are sent by the background worker. If it is not running, press Check now.

Set up (owners)How the AI takes orders

Customers do not fill in forms. They write naturally, for example: “2 black baseball caps and 1 medium navy polo shirt to 45 Temple Road, Kandy, I'm Nimal, cash on delivery”, in English, Sinhala, Tamil or Singlish. The AI pulls out every detail itself.

  1. It reads what is already there: products, quantity, size, colour, name, address, town and payment. It never asks again for what was said, and never asks for the phone number (it already knows it).
  2. It checks against your Store with a dry run (check_order): real prices, delivery fee and total, products that need a size or colour, out-of-stock items, a payment method that is not allowed in that town, orders above your limit. It asks once for everything still missing.
  3. It shows one summary and waits for the customer's “yes”. Only then is the order placed, by your own rules (see AI actions and approvals).
  4. It remembers the customer. Name, address, town and usual payment are saved. Next time the AI offers them in the same summary (“Deliver to your usual address at … in Kandy?”) instead of asking again. This memory is per business and per phone number.
  5. Changing an order: the AI looks at the current order, sends the complete new list, shows the new total, and changes it after the customer agrees. Orders that already shipped cannot be changed by the AI.

A WhatsApp location pin works as the delivery address. If a product has several colours, the AI asks for the colour (you can switch this off under Store > Store rules). Orders above the limit you set are handed to your team. Check how well it does with your own wording in Quality tests.

Daily workPayments, slips and invoices

For bank-transfer and card orders the customer often sends a photo of the slip. CaliChat reads it so your team can confirm payments faster, but a person always confirms the money.

  • Slip received: when a customer who owes a bank transfer sends an image that is a payment slip, CaliChat reads the amount, reference and bank, attaches it to the matching order, and moves the order to Payments to verify. You get an alert. If the amount differs from the order total, it says so, and the AI tells the customer the slip was received and will be checked, not that the payment is confirmed.
  • Verify: open Orders > Payments to verify, look at the slip, compare with your bank statement, and press Payment received (the order becomes Paid and the customer is thanked) or Reject (the customer is asked for another slip).
  • Invoices: every order has a printable invoice page on a secure link. On the order, press Open or Send link to customer (works within WhatsApp's 24-hour window). Customers can also ask the AI for their invoice. Put your address, phone and bank account under Store > Shown on invoices.

Daily workAppointments

  1. Open Appointments and add your services (name, length in minutes, price, how many can be booked at the same time).
  2. Free times follow your opening days and hours (Setup guide or Settings), with one hour of notice, up to 30 days ahead. Nobody can book the same time twice.
  3. Press Connect the store again if needed: when you have services, the AI gets tools to list services, offer free times, book and cancel. It offers two or three real free times, reads the date and time back in words, and books after the customer says yes.
  4. Turn on Appointment confirmation and Appointment reminder in Follow-ups. The reminder goes a set number of minutes before the start (default 3 hours) and is dropped if the booking was cancelled.
  5. On the day, mark visits Done or No-show. Team members need the “Book, complete and cancel appointments” permission.

Set up (owners)Buttons, photos and voice

Under Settings > How the assistant replies on WhatsApp:

Tap buttons and lists
Order summaries come with Yes and No buttons (in English, Sinhala or Tamil to match the customer). Product searches come as a pick-one list. A tap arrives as an ordinary message. Meta WhatsApp numbers only; Twilio, Messenger and Instagram get plain text.
Answer voice notes with a voice note
When a customer sends a voice note, short answers are spoken back (English, Sinhala, Tamil). Longer answers, answers with links, and anything that fails are sent as text. Needs ffmpeg on the server. It is off until you switch it on: listen to a few answers in your language first, and remember it uses extra AI tokens.
Product photos
Add an https image link to a product. When a customer asks to see it, the AI sends the photo on WhatsApp with the name and price.

Set up (owners)Ads, lead forms and catalogue import

  • Click-to-WhatsApp ads: when someone starts a chat from your ad, CaliChat remembers which ad. Reports shows how many chats each ad brought.
  • Facebook / Instagram lead forms: form answers become Leads automatically (name, phone, email, answers). Turn on New ad lead welcome in Follow-ups to message them; because they have never chatted with you, this needs an approved WhatsApp template, and filling the form counts as their consent. Your platform administrator must enable the leadgen webhook field in the Meta app.
  • Import products from WooCommerce or Shopify: Store > Import from WooCommerce or Shopify. It only reads from the other shop (names, prices, stock, sizes, colours, photos) and never writes back. Keys are stored encrypted and never shown again. Products without a stock count arrive with 100 in stock: set real numbers afterwards. Tick “Refresh every 6 hours” to keep it current.
  • SMS fallback: in Follow-ups enter your SMS provider's web address and body (Dialog, Mobitel, Text.lk, Notify.lk and similar offer an HTTP API), send a test, then tick “Send by SMS when WhatsApp cannot reach the customer” on the follow-ups that should use it. Promotional follow-ups still need the customer's opt-in.

OversightTeam access and labels

On Team, under Who can do what, owners choose for each member what they may see and change. Pages a member may not open disappear from their menu. Examples: let a manager edit products (“Edit products, delivery areas, store rules”) without making them an owner; hide full phone numbers from a part-time agent; let someone see only chats assigned to them.

  • Standard access is the starting set: Inbox, orders, leads, appointments, contacts, reports and read-only access to setup pages. Editing setup (store, templates, follow-ups, campaigns, knowledge) is off until an owner turns it on.
  • See every chat off: the member sees chats assigned to them plus unassigned ones, so they can pick up new work.
  • See full phone numbers off: numbers show as 94771****67 in the Inbox, orders, leads, contacts, appointments and downloads.
  • Inbox labels, pins and unread: in a chat you can add labels (owners create them), Pin up to 5 chats to the top, and Mark unread. The Inbox tabs include Unread and Pinned, and you can filter by label.

OversightReports

Reports shows the last 7, 30 or 90 days: revenue and orders (split into what the AI placed and what your team placed), how many chats ended in an order, the typical first reply time, how many chats the AI handled without a person, how conversations ended (counted from what the AI really did), where customers came from (direct, click-to-WhatsApp ads, best ads), best-selling products, the leads funnel and win rate, results per team member, appointments, and how each follow-up performed. Press Orders, Leads or Conversations under Download for a spreadsheet file. Cancelled orders never count as revenue.

OversightQuality tests

Before you trust a change to your agent (new instructions, new tools, a new AI model), try it on conversations you care about. Quality tests runs them with the real AI, using a made-up customer, so no real order or message is ever created. It costs AI tokens.

  1. Press Add starter tests: six ready-made checks (price question, no “order placed” without a yes, refund goes to a person, prompt injection ignored, unrelated requests declined, Sinhala answered in Sinhala).
  2. Write your own: one CUSTOMER: line per message, then EXPECT lines under it: tools and not_tools (which tools it must or must not use), reply_has and reply_lacks (words), handoff (yes or no), language (sinhala, tamil, english), max_chars.
  3. Press Run on one test or Run all. Each failed check is listed with the reason, and the last runs are kept so you can compare before and after a change.

Developers can run the same tests from the command line (php cli/agent_tests.php <tenant id>); it exits with an error code when a test fails.

Platform and ownersSign-up, plans and billing

  • Sign-up: new businesses open /signup, choose a plan and get a 14-day free trial with their own workspace. No card is taken. The platform administrator can close sign-up.
  • Plans: each plan sets the monthly AI replies, AI usage and team size. Owners see their usage under Plan & billing.
  • Paying: create an invoice for the plan and number of months, pay by bank transfer using the details shown, then press I have paid with the bank reference. The platform administrator checks the bank and confirms; the plan and the paid period start then.
  • Renewals: a renewal invoice appears a week before the period ends. If the trial or period ends unpaid there are 3 days of grace; after that the AI stops answering new chats (customers get your holding message and your team is alerted) until the invoice is paid.
  • Platform administrators: Billing lists payments waiting for confirmation, open invoices, every business's status, plan prices and limits, bank details, and lets you issue an invoice, restart a trial, cancel, or mark a business complimentary.

Set up (owners)Channels (WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram)

A channel links one Meta business account to CaliChat. You need a Meta developer app with the product enabled, and a public https address for CaliChat, because Meta must be able to reach it.

Add the channel in CaliChat

  1. Open Channels and choose the type.
  2. Enter a name and the ID: the Phone number ID (WhatsApp), the Page ID (Messenger) or the Instagram account ID.
  3. Paste the permanent access token and the Meta app secret. The secret is used to prove that messages really come from Meta; without it every message is rejected.
  4. Optionally pick which agent answers on this channel, then press Create channel.

Connect Meta to CaliChat

Set up (owners)One-click connect: Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp

Most businesses should not copy tokens by hand. When your platform administrator has switched on one-click connect, the top of the Channels page shows two buttons. You sign in to Facebook, choose what to share, and CaliChat sets up the webhooks for you.

Messenger and Instagram

  1. Press Connect Facebook Page & Instagram and sign in to Facebook with an account that is an admin of your Page.
  2. In the Facebook pop-up, select your Page and its linked Instagram account and accept the permissions to manage and read messages.
  3. Back in CaliChat, tick what to connect (Messenger on the Page, Instagram account) and press Connect selected.
  4. Send a test message to your Page or Instagram account from another profile. It appears in the Inbox.
Instagram checklist. The Instagram account must be a professional (business or creator) account, linked to your Facebook Page, and in the Instagram app under Settings → Messages and story replies → Message controls “Allow access to messages” must be on.

WhatsApp

  1. Press Connect WhatsApp number. A Facebook pop-up (Meta's Embedded Signup) opens.
  2. Sign in, then create or choose your WhatsApp Business Account, enter the business details and add or pick the phone number. Verify it with the code Meta sends by SMS or call.
  3. Finish the pop-up. CaliChat registers the number, subscribes to its messages and creates the channel.
Number rules. The number can receive a verification SMS or call, and if it is already used in the regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app, Meta will guide you through moving it. A number cannot be connected in two workspaces; CaliChat refuses a second attempt.

What happens behind the scenes

  • CaliChat stores the access tokens encrypted and never shows them again.
  • All messages arrive on one signed Meta webhook and are routed to your workspace by Page, Instagram account or phone number.
  • Press Verify connection on a channel card at any time. If a token is withdrawn (for example when you remove the app in Facebook settings), you get a “channel token” alert; just press Connect again.

For platform administrators: turning it on

  1. In Meta for Developers, create one app for the platform (type Business). Add Facebook Login for Business, Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp products.
  2. Open Platform → Settings in CaliChat, enter the App ID and App secret (and, for WhatsApp, the Embedded Signup configuration ID). Save.
  3. Copy three values from that page into the Meta app: the webhook callback URL with its verify token (subscribe the WhatsApp Business Account, Page and Instagram objects to messages), and the Valid OAuth Redirect URI.
  4. Your domain must be public https, with a privacy policy URL on the app.
Meta review is required before customers can use it. Until your app passes business verification and App Review (advanced access to the messaging permissions; Tech Provider status for WhatsApp Embedded Signup), one-click connect works only for people who have a role on the Meta app, such as you and your testers. That is a Meta rule that no software can skip; plan for it, and use the manual or Twilio route for pilots in the meantime.

Prefer to bring your own Meta app? A tenant can still connect manually (Phone number ID, Page ID, access token and app secret) using the form lower on the Channels page. Those channels keep their own webhook address.

Set up (owners)Testing with the Twilio WhatsApp Sandbox

A free Twilio trial lets you chat with CaliChat from your own WhatsApp within minutes, without waiting for a Meta business approval. Twilio calls CaliChat whenever you message its sandbox number, and CaliChat answers through Twilio.

What you need. A Twilio account (free trial), WhatsApp on your phone, a Gemini key in CaliChat, and a public https address for your computer. Twilio cannot reach localhost, so a tunnel such as ngrok is used while testing.

Part 1: Twilio

  1. Sign up for a Twilio trial and verify your phone number. On the Console home page, note the Account SID (starts with AC) and reveal the Auth Token.
  2. Open Messaging → Try it out → Send a WhatsApp message. Note the sandbox number (for example +1 415 523 8886) and your personal join code, a phrase like join sunny-tiger.
  3. On your phone, open WhatsApp and send that exact join message to the sandbox number. Twilio replies that you are connected. Only phones that joined can talk to the sandbox, and you may need to join again after a few days.

Part 2: make CaliChat reachable

  1. Install ngrok and run ngrok http 80 on the computer that runs CaliChat. Copy the https://…ngrok… address it shows.
  2. Open CaliChat through that address, for example https://abcd-12-34.ngrok-free.app/calichat, and sign in. Opening it this way makes CaliChat show the correct public callback URL. Optionally set APP_URL to the same address in .env.

Part 3: connect them

  1. In CaliChat open Channels, set Type to WhatsApp via Twilio, and fill in a name, the sandbox number, the Account SID and the Auth Token. Press Create channel.
  2. Copy the Callback URL on the new channel card.
  3. In Twilio, open the Sandbox settings and paste it into When a message comes in. Set the method to POST and save.
  4. Back in CaliChat press Verify connection. It should say “Connected as … (Trial, active)”.

  3. Send messages from your phone to the sandbox number and watch them appear in the Inbox.

Limits to know about

  • 24-hour window: you can reply freely for 24 hours after the customer's last message. Template messages are not supported on Twilio channels in CaliChat, so campaigns work only inside that window.
  • Sandbox is for testing. A live number needs a Twilio WhatsApp sender approved by Meta. When you get one, create another “WhatsApp via Twilio” channel with that number, or connect it through Meta directly.
  • Free tunnel addresses change each time ngrok restarts. Paste the new callback URL into Twilio again.
  • Security: CaliChat checks Twilio's signature on every message and rejects anything else. Your Auth Token is stored encrypted.
If nothing happens. Check the Twilio Console debugger first. A 403 there means the signature did not match: the Auth Token is wrong, or the URL in Twilio differs from the one CaliChat shows. No reply but messages appear in the Inbox means the AI or the send failed; see Monitoring and the channel card for the reason.

How your data is protected

  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Tokens, secrets and API keys are encrypted and never shown again.

HelpFAQ

Does the AI ever reply without my approval?

Yes, that is its purpose. You control what it knows, which systems it can read, and when it must hand over. Supervise in the Inbox and the Monitoring page, and test changes in the Playground first.

Can customers see another customer's orders?

Not if your order tool uses a contact.phone parameter. The lookup is forced to the verified sender, whatever the customer types. Always test with a different number.

What happens when the AI service is down?

CaliChat retries, switches to a backup model, and after several attempts sends your fallback message and hands the chat to your team. You get an alert.

Can I stop the AI for one customer only?

Yes. Open the chat and press Take over. Only that conversation is paused.

Does it understand voice notes and photos?

It converts them to text first, then answers. Quality depends on audio clarity. Photos are described and any visible text is read. This is a newer feature, so check a few real examples.

How much does it cost to run?

Your plan sets monthly limits. The Monitoring page shows an estimated cost from list prices your administrator entered. Treat it as a guide and check the real figures with your AI provider.

Is there a mobile app?

No app, but the website works on phones, including the inbox.

Can I use my own Gemini key?

Yes, in Settings. Your usage then bills to your own account. Leave it empty to use the platform key.
